# Settings

Choose **Settings** from Home. The available options are grouped by what they
change during everyday use.

## Connection

- **Connection setup** starts the Local, Remote, or Both / automatic setup.
- **Connection profile** edits saved Local and Remote PCServer addresses and the
  active mode.
- **HTTP proxy** configures the optional generic proxy used when the network
  requires it. Leave it empty for a normal direct connection.

## Audio

- **Audio gain** adjusts playback loudness.
- **Audio languages** sets preferred audio-language order or restores manual
  selection.

## Subtitles

- **Subtitle languages** sets preferred subtitle-language order.
- **Subtitle size** selects a readable subtitle scale from 1.0x through 3.0x;
  1.5x is the default.
- **Matroska subtitle limit** controls how many tracks per language may be
  packaged for selectable subtitle playback.

Settings are saved in the PocketCinema app configuration and are separate from
the Tailscale companion's settings.
