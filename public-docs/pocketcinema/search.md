# Search

1. Choose **Search** from the Home screen.
2. Enter part or all of a title.
3. Select a result. Results identify their type as **MOVIE** or **SERIES**.

Selecting a movie opens its actions. Selecting a series opens its seasons and
episodes. If there are no results, try a shorter title or check the PCServer
connection and account/service setup.
