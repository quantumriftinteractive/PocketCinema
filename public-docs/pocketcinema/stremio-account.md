# Stremio accounts and addons

Open the PocketCinema **Stremio Account** screen and follow its account-linking flow. Use the official Stremio approval process; PocketCinema does not need your Stremio email or password.

Once linked, PCServer discovers the account’s installed addons and normalizes their manifests. Search, metadata, streams, and compatible Live TV catalogs can then use those account-backed sources. Addons are not bundled into PocketCinema, and no particular addon or country is required.

Account credentials, addon configuration, and upstream URLs stay on PCServer. If an addon is unavailable or returns an unsupported source, it may not appear or may not be playable on the Miyoo.
